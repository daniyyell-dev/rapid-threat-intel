    How it works

    From report to hunt in one step

    1. 01

      Collected

      AI watches vendor research, government advisories and abuse feeds around the clock and pulls out every indicator and behaviour.

    2. 02

      Verified by an analyst

      Each entry is checked against its sources. Shared services and private ranges never reach a block list, and every row cites where it came from.

    3. 03

      Ready to hunt or block

      Download block lists, or run the same hunt in Defender XDR, Splunk or Google SecOps. Behaviours come too, so you can find what indicators miss.

    Block lists

    Block with confidence

    High-confidence IPs, domains, URLs and hashes, grouped by threat or split by type. Import straight into Defender XDR, or point your firewall at the plain-text lists.

    • Every entry cites its sources
    • Shared and private infrastructure is never listed
    • Updated as each new threat lands

    Hunts

    One hunt, three platforms

    Each threat ships the same hunt for Defender XDR (KQL), Splunk (SPL) and Google SecOps (YARA-L), with behaviours mapped to real log fields. Run it before you block to find past activity.

    • Paste and run, no lookups to build
    • Low-noise, written for SOC triage
    • Behaviour leads alongside indicators

            About IOCWatch

            AI-powered threat intelligence, verified by an analyst. Not a full feed: only confirmed, high-impact threats with published indicators, researched and ready to deploy.

            Danger: Handle every indicator as live and malicious

            • Never visit, open, download or resolve them from a work or personal device.
            • Analyse them only in an isolated sandbox, hosted like ANY.RUN or self-hosted like OpenSandbox.
            • Follow your organisation’s security policies. For defensive use only.
